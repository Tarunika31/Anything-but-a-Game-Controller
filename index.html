How to play

  1. Scan the room QR code.
  2. Tap Enable motion on your phone.
  3. Hold the phone flat and tap Calibrate.
  4. Tilt to move, reach the green goal.

Motion sensors require HTTPS on deployed sites (localhost is allowed for local testing).
